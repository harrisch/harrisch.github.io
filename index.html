About

I've been building things with computers since long before anyone paid me to. The story so far has run from mobile apps and game backends to one of the world's largest Rails deployments — and eventually to content infrastructure at Box, where I helped design the representations API for fetching platform-native renditions of user content and contributed to the launch of Box Shield.

Today I'm at Indeed in Tokyo, working on a Kafka-heavy backend — Indeed's job sponsorship pipeline — that keeps sponsored jobs flowing across the world's largest job site. I'm always looking forward to learning something new; lately that's meant AI agents and home automation.

Work
